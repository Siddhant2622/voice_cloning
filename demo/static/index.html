      🛡️ System Architecture & Methodology

      VoiceGuard employs a multi-tiered forensic pipeline combining client-side Edge DSP feature extractors with server-side Dual-SSL neural classifiers inspired by Resemble AI DETECT-2B.

      • Dual-SSL Acoustic Embeddings: WavLM + Wav2Vec2 cross-encoder captures phase discontinuity and vocoder artifacts.
      • Mamba-SSM Temporal Modeling: Captures unnatural prosody rhythm and pitch micro-tremors over long sequences.
      • Bio-Acoustic Liveness: Inspects vocal tract formant damping, respiration pauses, and sub-band spectral rolloff.
      • Bayesian Evidence Fusion: Re-calibrated log-odds fusion guarantees zero false alerts on genuine human conversations.
